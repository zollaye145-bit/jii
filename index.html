How a custom order works

No pattern to read, no yarn to buy. You describe the piece, she does the rest.

  1. Design it in the studio

    Choose the piece, your size or exact measurements, the stitch pattern and up to three yarn colors. The preview and price update as you go.

  2. Send it on WhatsApp

    Your design arrives with every detail filled in. Jihane confirms the price and the date, and you pay a small deposit to start.

  3. She crochets it by hand

    You get progress photos along the way. When it is finished, it is delivered to your door or ready for pickup.

Jihane

She learned to crochet as a girl and never put the hook down. What started as gifts for family became Ji Little Moments: a small studio where every piece is made by one pair of hands.

Each sweater takes days, some blankets take weeks. Nothing is made in advance in a factory size — it is made for the person who will wear it, in the colors they chose, and finished with the same care whether it is a beanie or a bed blanket.
